## Introduction

The Amazon KDP marketplace offers incredible opportunities for self-publishers. However, the difference between an author making $50 and a six-figure publishing business is **Niche Selection**.

## Niche 1: Somatic Anxiety Workbooks

Mental wellness and nervous system regulation books continue to dominate non-fiction bestseller lists. Buyers actively seek practical, guided exercises rather than mere theory.

### Why It Works:
- High perceived reader value ($12.99–$16.99 price point)
- High keyword search velocity with moderate competition saturation
- Strong organic word-of-mouth recommendations

## Niche 2: Geometric Color by Number Mosaics

Stress-relief activity books for adults have evolved. Numbered geometric plates provide structured relaxation for non-artists.

- Estimated monthly royalties: $3,000–$7,500/book
- Target audience: Adult mindfulness practitioners and seniors

## Conclusion

Focusing on validated buyer search intent before drafting a manuscript is the highest ROI activity for any indie author in 2026.
